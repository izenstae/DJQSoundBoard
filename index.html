How do sounds get saved?

Sounds you add with + Add sounds are saved in this browser on this device, so they're still here next time (even offline). They don't sync to other devices — use Download backup and Restore to move them.

To make a sound permanent for everyone who opens the site, upload the file into the sounds/ folder of the GitHub repo. It shows up automatically after the site redeploys.
